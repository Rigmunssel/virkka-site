Company

Virkka is a software company based in Espoo, Finland. We work with companies that want to automate demanding, rule-heavy work or bring their website up to date, and with medical device manufacturers that need regulatory documentation for the EU market.

The founders' previous work includes developing machine learning applications, designing and operating profitable algorithmic trading systems, and building software products used by thousands of people every day.

We take projects from the first specification to a system in production, and we can maintain the result after delivery.
