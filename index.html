The public-data sections of your PSUR, prepared for you

For EU MDR manufacturers of Class IIa and IIb devices. We prepare sections (b) to (d) of your Periodic Safety Update Report from public sources, in the MDCG 2022-21 template format, ready to review and paste.

What you receive

MDCG 2022-21 Annex I · PMS information (b)

Scientific literature review

Search strategy, screening counts and the relevant publications on your device type for the data collection period, each with its finding type and link.

MDCG 2022-21 Annex I · PMS information (c)

Public databases and registry data

Adverse-event reports, recalls and field safety notices for similar devices from the FDA, Health Canada and the UK MHRA, with reports about unrelated products and your own reports removed.

MDCG 2022-21 Annex I · PMS information (d)

Publicly available information about similar devices

Similar devices on the EU market, new devices cleared in the USA with links to their published summaries, conference findings, and the SSCP statement.

Delivered as a Word file in your PSUR structure, with the query behind every figure so your team or notified body can re-run it. Sales, complaints, your own vigilance data and the benefit-risk conclusion stay with you.

Why it matters

MDCG 2025-10 asks manufacturers to actively seek publicly available information about similar devices. Your notified body audits how your post-market surveillance plan is applied at least once every 12 months.
