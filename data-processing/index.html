Data Processing and Confidentiality Statement

How we handle the documents and data you send us for a service.

Version 1.1 · Last updated 20 October 2026

At a glance

  • We use your documents only to deliver the service you ordered, and keep them confidential.
  • For personal data in your documents, you are the controller and we are your processor under Article 28 GDPR. We sign your NDA or a data processing agreement on request.
  • We delete your documents within 30 days of delivery and confirm the deletion in writing on request.
  • You upload to EUDAMED yourself. We never ask for your EU Login or EUDAMED credentials.
  • We notify you of a security incident without undue delay, and no later than 72 hours after we become aware of it.

1. About this statement

This statement describes how Virkka Oy handles the documents and data that clients send us for regulatory services, such as EUDAMED legacy device registration and post-market surveillance data. If we have signed a non-disclosure agreement or data processing agreement with you, that agreement prevails over this statement where the two differ.

3. Definitions

Client MaterialsDocuments and data you send us, such as price lists, product lists, certificates, declarations of conformity, instructions for use and labels.
DeliverablesWhat we produce for you from the Client Materials, such as EUDAMED upload files, review workbooks and reports.
Personal DataInformation about an identified or identifiable person, as defined in Article 4 of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).
ServicesThe work you have ordered from us.

4. Purpose limitation

We use Client Materials only to deliver the Services. We do not use them for marketing, for work for other clients, or to build data sets for anyone else.

5. Confidentiality

We treat Client Materials and Deliverables as confidential. Everyone at Virkka who works on your project is bound by confidentiality. We disclose Client Materials only to the sub-processors in section 10, as far as needed to deliver the Services, or where the law requires it. On request we sign your non-disclosure agreement before you send anything.

6. Our role under the GDPR

Client Materials may contain Personal Data, such as the names, signatures or contact details of your staff. For that data you are the controller and Virkka Oy is the processor. We process it only on your documented instructions and in line with Article 28(3) GDPR. On request we sign a data processing agreement based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses between controllers and processors (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/915), and we help you respond to requests from data subjects. For the business contact details we use to communicate with you, Virkka Oy is the controller, as described in the privacy notice at the foot of this website.

7. Patient and health data

Our Services do not need patient data or health data of any person. Please remove such data before sending documents. If we receive it, we delete it and tell you.

8. Use of AI tools

We use AI software to read Client Materials and extract the data a Deliverable needs. We process Client Materials only through AI services used under commercial terms that include a data processing agreement and do not allow the provider to train its models on our clients' content. We do not use consumer AI apps for Client Materials. Every value in a Deliverable is linked to the document it came from, and we check Deliverables before we send them.

9. Security measures

  • Access to Client Materials is limited to the people at Virkka working on your project.
  • Our email and file storage provider encrypts data in transit and at rest.
  • We never place Client Materials on a public website or share them through public links.

12. Security incidents

If we become aware of a breach affecting Client Materials or Personal Data, we notify you without undue delay, and no later than 72 hours after we become aware of it, with the information you need to meet your own obligations.

13. Supplier qualification and audits

We complete supplier qualification questionnaires for your quality management system on request, and provide the information you need to show that our processing meets Article 28 GDPR.

14. Your control over EUDAMED

You upload EUDAMED files yourself, with your own EU Login. We never ask for your EU Login or EUDAMED credentials and never submit anything to EUDAMED on your behalf.

15. Changes to this statement

We publish each new version on this page with its date. We tell clients with an active project about material changes before they apply to their Client Materials.

16. Governing law, complaints and contact

This statement is governed by Finnish law. You may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland (tietosuoja.fi). For questions, deletion requests, or to sign an NDA or data processing agreement, email ville.palola@virkka.com.
