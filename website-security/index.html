Services

Security review

A check of what anyone on the internet can see about your website, and where it is exposed.

  • HTTPS certificate and configuration
  • Security headers and cookies
  • Outdated software, plugins and themes
  • Exposed files, backups and admin pages

Email domain security

Your SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, which stop others from sending phishing emails in your company's name.

Recommendations

A written report that explains each finding in plain language, how serious it is, and what to change first.

Redesign and rebuild

A new website that loads quickly, works well on phones and is easier to keep secure, built around your content.

We review only what is publicly visible, unless you give written permission for deeper testing.
